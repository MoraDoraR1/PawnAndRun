# Pawn & Run UI Style Guide

## Palette

- Espresso chassis: `#211A14`
- Espresso icon/text: `#3A2F22`
- Bronze keyline: `#B5772E`
- Cream face: `#FFFAF0`
- Cream gold: `#E9DE9A`
- Board green: `#4C7A5E`
- Ready green: `#5FA83B`
- Settings teal: `#2FA6A6`
- Close red: `#9C3B32`

## Geometry

- Outer corner radius: approximately 21% of the short side
- Bronze frame width: approximately 4% of the short side
- Inner face inset: approximately 10% of the short side
- Icon weight: approximately 12–15% of the icon canvas
- Decorative chess-tile brackets are the only ornamental motif

## Lighting

- Controls use a restrained highlight from the upper center.
- Paired chess pieces mirror their highlights toward the composition center.
- Do not introduce unrelated drop shadows, neon glows, or plastic gloss.
